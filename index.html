I'M NAGUIB — 3D ARTIST · CHARACTERS & ENVIRONMENTS · ANIMATED FILM

SUBJECT: NAGUIB_A · FRAME 01

I grew up in Giza, surrounded by the magic and mystery of ancient and modern Egypt. I started as a street artist and calligraphy painter — which is why I went to France to study the magic of 3D, so I could tell stories through animated film.

“I STARTED ON WALLS — NOW I BUILD THE WORLDS BEHIND THE CAMERA.”

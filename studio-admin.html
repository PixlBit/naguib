        AI the generated work on the home page

        The link is the row. Paste a different URL over it and that row becomes that video — that is how you edit one. Vimeo, YouTube, Instagram and X all work, and they are formatted identically. + ADD adds one, REMOVE takes one off.

        A video may sit here and in PROJECTS at the same time — nothing stops it, because being generated and being client work are not exclusive. Instagram and X publish no thumbnail, so those cards draw their own artwork unless you upload a frame; Vimeo and YouTube bring their own. A cover is scaled to 720×1280 in this browser before it is sent.
